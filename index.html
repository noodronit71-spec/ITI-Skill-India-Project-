Discover Vibrant Gujarat

Gujarat, Located On The Western Coast Of India, Is A Land Of Rich Cultural Heritage, Ancient Architecture, Sacred Temples, Diverse Wildlife, And Breathtaking Natural Landscapes. From Vast White Salt Deserts And Royal Palaces To The World's Tallest Statue And The Last Natural Habitat Of The Asiatic Lion, Gujarat Offers Unforgettable Experiences For Every Traveler.

Top 5 Tourist Destinations

  • Statue of Unity (Kevadia): Standing at 182 Meters, This Monumental Tribute To Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Is The World's Tallest Statue, Surrounded By Gardens, Museums, And Narmada River Views.
    Statue of Unity
  • Somnath Temple (Veraval): One Of The Twelve Sacred Jyotirlinga Shrines Of Lord Shiva, Perched Dramatically Along The Arabian Sea Coast.
    Somnath Temple
  • Great Rann of Kutch: A Mesmerizing Expanse Of White Salt Desert Famous For Its Moonlit Beauty And The Vibrant Annual Rann Utsav Cultural Festival.
    Great Rann of Kutch
  • Rani ki Vav (Patan): An Intricately Sculpted 11th-Century Stepwell And UNESCO World Heritage Site Showcasing Remarkable Solanki-Era Craftsmanship.
    Rani ki Vav
  • Gir National Park (Sasan Gir): The Only Natural Habitat In The World Where Visitors Can Spot Majestic Asiatic Lions In The Wild.
    Gir National Park
